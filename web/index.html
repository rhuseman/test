      How the tiles are judged. Preloaded joint: load factor nL = (SpAt − Fi)/(C·P) (Shigley eq. 8-28), fatigue and separation fail below 1.0, show a low margin from 1.0 to 1.5, and are OK at 1.5 and above. Proof and yield only need 1.0, because the preload puts the bolt near proof strength on purpose. Bare bolt: yield, proof, ultimate and fatigue use the same 1.0 and 1.5 bands. Apply your own design code's margins.

      Limits: calculated C assumes a through-bolt with nut, both members the same material, and standard thread length. Endurance strengths for SAE 1, 2 and ISO 4.6, 4.8, 5.8, and for all cut threads, are estimates scaled from Shigley Tables 8-16 and 8-17. Tension only: no shear, bending or finite-life fatigue. Verify results before using them for design.
